Privacy

Last updated September 22, 2026

The short version

Daysake does not collect your data. We have no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Where your data lives

Your names, dates, photos, moments and notes are stored on your iPhone and in your own iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit). We can't see them. Apple handles that storage under Apple's privacy policy.

Linking with a partner

If you invite your partner, the counter, moments and special dates are shared with them through iCloud sharing. Your "Little things" notes are never shared. You can stop sharing at any time from Settings → Start over, or from the invite screen.

Notifications

Milestone alerts are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

Optional AI features

If a future version offers optional writing and planning help, the text you choose to send for that request will be processed to produce the answer and not used for advertising. This policy will be updated before any such feature ships.

Children

Daysake is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Settings → Start over deletes everything from your device and iCloud. Deleting the app removes local data; you can also remove Daysake data in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.
