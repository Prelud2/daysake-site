How do I link with my partner?

Tap Invite on the Us screen and send the link. Your partner opens it on their iPhone and Daysake opens with your shared counter. Both of you need to be signed in to iCloud.

How do I add a widget?

Long-press the Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget and search for Daysake. All widgets are free.

I'm not getting milestone alerts.

Check iOS Settings → Notifications → Daysake, and Settings → Alerts in the app. Alerts arrive at the time you pick (9 AM by default).

Is day 1 the day we got together?

By default, yes. You can change that in Settings → "Count that day as day 1".
